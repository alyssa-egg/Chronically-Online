## How to approve or suggest changes

- Review each question once in **Question archive**. Its scheduled dates are listed together. Edit the Decision, Niche idea, and Source columns using GitHub’s pencil button.
- Use **Exact daily questions and choices** to see all four quizzes for a date, including the actual A–D choice order. Each date expands separately.
- Review **Anonymous Ask puzzles** for answers, aliases, and all six clues. Edit the review fields beneath each puzzle.
- Decisions: **Needs review**, **Keep**, **Replace**, or **Rewrite**. For a one-day change, include the date in your note.
- Or reply in chat: “October 11 / main quiz / Q2 — replace with [reference].” Original public uploads and a suggested correct answer help; rough ideas are welcome.

**Rotation:** each day has 10 main questions, 5 Tumblr questions, 5 YouTube questions, 5 New Lore questions, and one Ask. The quiz content repeats every six days: October 17 repeats October 11. All 30 Asks are different. Quiz/Ask content cycles together again on November 10; choice order may change. New content or scheduling changes will change this plan.
