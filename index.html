A NOTE FROM THE ASKBOX

Your fandom phase
was never a phase.

The Fandom Files is a daily fandom game and a home for the internet eras that raised your feed. You don’t have to know everything to belong here.

Today’s Deep Cut is one free question, four choices, and three optional clues. One answer locks your round; each clue costs 20 points. It rotates from our prepared archive at your local midnight. Your answer and score stay in this browser. No account is needed.

Open the three era archives to uncover short references, follow sources, and save discoveries in My Files. Collect stamps as you play and explore.

The original ten-question quiz, Anonymous Ask, Lost Post, Sideblog, collectibles, and era game collections are under “more to explore.” Those original daily games reset at midnight Eastern, and completing them stamps your reading diary. Existing free folders, trials, and the $5 one-time Era Pass remain available for the game collections. The new era discovery pages are free.

Little chimes are made from scratch, and you can mute them anytime. No celebrity clips or licensed songs. Activity counts stay on your device; there’s no public leaderboard.

The Fandom Files is an independent fan-culture game, not affiliated with any featured artist, creator, or platform.
