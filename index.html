A NOTE FROM THE ASKBOX

Your fandom phase
was never a phase.

The Fandom Files is a daily fandom game and a home for the internet eras that raised your feed. You don’t have to know everything to belong here.

The Daily File has three interactive rounds: Decode the Screenshot, Rewind, and The Soundtrack. Everyone on the same local calendar date gets the same three files. Uncover original recreated artifacts, order five dated events, and guess songs from factual text clues. No licensed audio, lyrics or real user posts are used.

Open the three era archives to uncover short references, follow sources, and save discoveries in My Files. Collect stamps as you play and explore.

The Daily File is free, with no account required. It saves in this browser, has no time limit, and pauses the clock between rounds. Reveal costs and unsuccessful guesses reduce the points available; consecutive successful rounds earn combo bonuses. Practice lets you replay each mechanic without changing your daily score. The new game’s shared leaderboard and three-life Endless Archive are planned for later phases. Earlier games, the original Infinite Archive, its account-linked scores, Sideblog, purchases and Memories remain accessible.

Little chimes are made from scratch, and you can mute them anytime. No celebrity clips or licensed songs. Activity counts stay on your device; the new Daily File has no shared ranking yet.

The Fandom Files is an independent fan-culture game, not affiliated with any featured artist, creator, or platform.
