A NOTE FROM THE ASKBOX

Your fandom phase
was never a phase.

The Fandom Files is a daily fandom game and a home for the internet eras that raised your feed. You don’t have to know everything to belong here.

The Daily Connection is a daily grouping puzzle: 16 tiles, four specific connections, and room for four mix-ups. Select four tiles and file your guess. Category notes explain the references after the file closes. New puzzles open at your local midnight.

Open the three era archives to uncover short references, follow sources, and save discoveries in My Files. Collect stamps as you play and explore.

The daily file is free, with no account required. The Daily Connection records elapsed time without a time limit. Three era personality quizzes are untimed too. Earlier games, including the timed Deep Cut, remain in the quiz extras. Your completed results, history, streaks and stamps live in this browser; they do not sync between devices. The original games, Sideblog, existing purchases and Memories remain accessible from Quizzes or My Files.

Little chimes are made from scratch, and you can mute them anytime. No celebrity clips or licensed songs. Activity counts stay on your device; there’s no public leaderboard.

The Fandom Files is an independent fan-culture game, not affiliated with any featured artist, creator, or platform.
